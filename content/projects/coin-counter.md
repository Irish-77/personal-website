With our Coin Detector App you can take a picture of your Euro coins. The AI, trained on custom-labeled data, calculates the cumulative value of the coins in the picture (we published our dataset on Kaggle). The app takes the photo, which is sent to the backend. The AI (Faster R-CNN with MobileNet Backbone) then calculates the value of the coins, and the recognized coins are labeled in the app. Using Ngrok, the home computer can be used as a server. *The app development was not my area of responsibility.*
